How to read this

A forecast is not a pick

"Spain 53%" means Spain loses this tournament almost half the time. If they do, the forecast didn't fail — the less likely thing happened, which is most of what happens in football. And a wrinkle worth sitting with: Spain is the most likely champion, yet in the single most likely final (vs Argentina) the model has Argentina by a nose, 51.5 to 48.5. Both are true. Aggregated probability and single-path probability answer different questions, and confusing them is the most common way forecasts get misread — in sports and everywhere else.
